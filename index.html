How to Play

  • Listen for phrases, topics, or references during the sessions.
  • Tap a square when you hear or see it to mark it.
  • The center square is a free space.
  • Get five in a row — across, down, or diagonal — to win.
  • Continue playing to earn additional bingos or fill your card for a blackout bingo!
